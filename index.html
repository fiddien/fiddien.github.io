About

I'm Ilma, an AI technical lead at AssistX in Jakarta, building NLP applications. I care just as much about the question behind the work: what should these systems do, and for whom?

I studied Mathematics at Institut Teknologi Bandung, then Speech and Language Processing at the University of Edinburgh.

That question became a book. With Aditya Firman Ihsan, I wrote Menyikapi Era Kecerdasan Artifisial: Etika dan Tata Kelola (ITB Press, September 2026), which looks at AI through ethics and governance for Indonesian readers. Earlier, I contributed chapters to Sains Tidak Sepasti Itu and Sains (Masih) Tidak Sepasti Itu to bring philosophy of science into everyday conversation.

This blog is where I think in public about technology, ethics, and human development. My thesis and other academic work are archived on Academia, and the more casual writing is on Medium.

For my work history, find me on LinkedIn.
