I'm Ilma, an engineer in Jakarta building AI-powered solutions for enterprise. I care just as much about the question behind the work: what should these systems do, and for whom?

I studied Mathematics at Institut Teknologi Bandung, then Speech and Language Processing at the University of Edinburgh.

That question became a book. I co-wrote [*Menyikapi Era Kecerdasan Artifisial: Etika dan Tata Kelola*](https://www.itbpress.id/menyikapi-era-kecerdasan-artifisial-etika-dan-tata-kelola) (ITB Press, September 2026), which looks at AI through ethics and governance for Indonesian readers. Earlier, I contributed chapters to [*Sains Tidak Sepasti Itu*](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/218634463-sains-tidak-sepasti-itu) and [*Sains (Masih) Tidak Sepasti Itu*](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/239558692.Sains_masih_tidak_sepasti_itu) to bring philosophy of science into everyday conversation.

[This blog](/posts) is where I think in public about technology, ethics, and human development. My thesis and other academic work are archived on [Academia](https://independent.academia.edu/IlmaAliyaFiddien). [Medium](https://fiddien.medium.com) holds my more personal essays, in Indonesian and English, on learning, science, faith, and the quiet moments in between.

For my work history, find me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/fiddien).
